# STATS 5101 Project 

The H-1B visa program is a non-immigrant visa program that lets US employers employ foreign nationals in specialty occupations. A specialty occupation is one that requires the application of a body of highly specialized knowledge and the attainment of at least a bachelor’s degree or its equivalent. The intent of the H-1B provisions is to help employers who cannot otherwise obtain needed business skills and abilities from the U.S. workforce by authorizing the temporary employment of qualified individuals who are not otherwise authorized to work in the United States. 

Understanding patterns of H-1B approvals and denials across employers, states, and years has significant implications for workforce planning, immigration policy, and economic analysis. This project analyzes H-1B employer petition data spanning 2021 to 2023 to identify key predictors of petition outcomes and to assess whether approval trends have shifted meaningfully over this three-year period.
